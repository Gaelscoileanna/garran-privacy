Garrán Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What Garrán is

Garrán is an offline ogham app. It offers ogham draws, deity and story pages, herbal information, and a personal journal, all on your device.

What we do not collect

Garrán does not collect, store, or share your personal information. It does not use accounts, tracking, analytics, or advertising.

What stays on your device

Your journal entries, draws, settings, and daily seeds are stored only on your device. If you export your journal, that export file stays with you.

What Garrán does not send

Garrán does not send your data to a server. The app works offline, and your journal and draws are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Contact

If you contact the developer through GitHub, that contact is governed by GitHub's own policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, and the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
